Stockz is a launchpad on BNB Chain. Every token you create is paired with a tokenized stock, like Tesla or Nvidia, and stands as a building on a living pixel city map.

▼ How it works

What is Stockz?

🚀

A launchpad

Create a token in about a minute: name, ticker, image, done. No code and no contracts to write.

📈

Paired with stocks

Your token trades against a tokenized stock instead of BNB. 88 stocks and ETFs are available, from TSLAB and NVDAB to QQQB and SPYB.

🏙️

A living city

Every token is a building standing on its stock's plot. It grows with its holders and falls to ruins when trading stops.

How it works

  1. 1

    Connect your wallet

    MetaMask, Trust, OKX or Binance Wallet, on BNB Smart Chain.

  2. 2

    Pick 1 Pair or Multi-pair

    Choose one stock, or several, or all of them.

  3. 3

    Fill in your token

    Name, ticker, image, a short description and your links.

  4. 4

    Sign and launch

    Confirm in your wallet. Your building appears on the map within a minute.

1 Pair or Multi-pair

1 Pair

1 token · 1 stock

The simplest launch. One transaction, one building on one stock's plot.

Multi-pair

1 token · many stocks

Your token, with the same name and image, launched on every stock you pick. Each pair is a real token on-chain, so your project stands in many districts at once. You confirm once per stock.

Your building grows

Tent when launched, House at 25 holders, Shop at 100, Tower at 300, and a Landmark for the most popular token. Fireworks mean trading right now. No trades for 12 hours turns a building into ruins.

Built on BNB Chain

Network

BNB Smart Chain mainnet. Every launch is a real on-chain transaction.

Tokens

Standard BEP-20 tokens, created through an established token launch contract.

Stock pairs

Tokenized stock tokens on BNB Chain, such as TSLAB (Tesla) and NVDAB (Nvidia).

Live map

The city reads launches, holders and trades straight from the chain and updates every minute.

Safe by design

🔑

You sign everything

Stockz never holds your keys or your funds.

🧪

Simulated first

Every launch is tested before your wallet opens. A failing launch never costs BNB.

✅

Exact approvals

If you add an initial buy, you approve that exact amount. Never unlimited.
